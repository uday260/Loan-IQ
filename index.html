💡 Prepayment Tips
01.Early prepayments save more — the first 5 years have the highest interest component.
02.Even ₹50,000 per year can reduce your tenure by 3–5 years on a ₹50L loan.
03.Choose "reduce tenure" over "reduce EMI" — you save significantly more interest.
04.No tax benefit on principal repayment beyond Section 80C's ₹1.5L limit.

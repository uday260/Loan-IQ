💡 Tips
01.Prepayments in the first 5 years save the most — interest is highest early on.
02.₹50,000/year can cut a 20-year loan by 3–5 years.
03.Choose Reduce Tenure — saves far more than Reduce EMI.
04.RBI: No prepayment penalty on floating-rate home loans.
📐 EMI Formula
EMI = P × R × (1+R)^N
─────────────────
(1+R)^N − 1

P = principal, R = monthly rate (annual ÷ 1200), N = total months
